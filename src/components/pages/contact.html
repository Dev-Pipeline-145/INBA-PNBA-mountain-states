Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our competitions and registration

INBA (International Natural Bodybuilding Association) and PNBA (Professional Natural Bodybuilding Association) are both drug-free bodybuilding organizations. INBA focuses on amateur competitions, while PNBA handles professional-level events. We host both types of competitions.

Preparation typically takes 12-16 weeks and includes diet, training, posing practice, and tanning. We recommend working with a coach experienced in natural bodybuilding. Check our resources page for detailed preparation guides.

We offer Men's Physique, Women's Bikini, Classic Physique, Bodybuilding, and Wellness divisions. Each division has specific criteria and judging standards. Check our rules page for detailed information about each division.

Yes, all competitors must pass drug testing to compete. We use polygraph testing and random drug screening to ensure fair, natural competition. This is what makes our competitions truly natural bodybuilding events.
