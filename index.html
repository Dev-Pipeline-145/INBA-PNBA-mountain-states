Coach David Reynolds

Contest Prep & Nutrition Specialist

With over 15 years of experience in natural bodybuilding, Coach Reynolds has helped dozens of athletes achieve their pro cards. His holistic approach focuses on sustainable training methods and evidence-based nutrition protocols.

Amanda Chen

Women's Physique & Figure Expert

Former INBA pro athlete turned coach, Amanda specializes in helping women achieve their competitive goals while maintaining health and balance. Her programs emphasize proper posing, stage presence, and mental preparation.

Marcus Williams

Strength & Conditioning Coach

Marcus brings his background in exercise science to natural bodybuilding preparation. His training philosophy centers on building a strong foundation through compound movements and progressive overload.

Lisa Patel

Online Coaching & Peak Week Specialist
