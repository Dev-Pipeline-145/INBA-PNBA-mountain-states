Registration Information

Everything you need to know about competing in our shows

Eligibility

  • Must be 18+ years old
  • Drug-free for minimum 7 years
  • Valid government ID required
  • Medical clearance certificate

Divisions

  • Men's Physique
  • Women's Bikini
  • Classic Physique
  • Bodybuilding
  • Wellness

Registration Fees

  • Early Bird: $75 (until 6 weeks before)
  • Regular: $95 (until 2 weeks before)
  • Late: $125 (final week)
  • Additional divisions: $25 each

What's Included

  • Competition entry
  • Drug testing
  • Judging panel
  • Awards ceremony
  • Photo opportunities
